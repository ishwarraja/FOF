FOF CHARACTER PRODUCTION BUNDLE
=================================

Contents
--------
FOF_All_12_Character_Detail_Slides.pptx
    14-slide deck: cover + 12 character detail slides + production matrix/refined prompt.

audio/
    12 original 20-second theme DEMO WAV files. These are proof-of-concept musical motifs, not final studio recordings.

FOF_character_specs.json
    Machine-readable source data for the 12 character specifications.

FOF_special_move_matrix.csv
    Special move count, names, commands and target animation timing.

Important
---------
The deck is a character-production specification. The uploaded Arjun Rao image is used as the visual reference on the cover; it is not treated as a source for the other characters.
The audio files are original synthetic demo motifs intended to establish direction. Final orchestration, voice acting, foley and mastered game audio should be produced during the audio-production stage.

Gameplay baseline
-----------------
- Target combat update: 60 FPS.
- Standard roster: 4 Special Moves + 1 Super + 1 Ultimate.
- Victor Kane: 5 Special Moves + 1 Super + 1 Ultimate as final boss.
- Animation lists cover locomotion, normals, special moves, super/ultimate, reactions and presentation states.

IP / originality
----------------
FOF should use original character designs, costumes, voices, music, sound effects and assets. Do not copy KOF/SNK, Street Fighter/Capcom or other copyrighted character designs, animations, music or assets. The game can take inspiration from the general fighting-game genre while maintaining its own visual identity and mechanics.
